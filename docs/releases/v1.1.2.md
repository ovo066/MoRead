## 中文

### 阅读排版与段评

- 中文行首、行尾禁则统一应用于 TXT 与 EPUB 排版，闭合标点随前文移动时不会挤出安全区；成对符号和西文单词内部不再被错误拉开。
- 段评不再参与正文分页占位，新增或删除段评不会改变字距、行高和分页；进入阅读页或从后台恢复时，已生成的 AI 段评会在当前页及时出现。
- EPUB 图片支持长按预览、缩放、拖动、旋转、复位、导出和定位原文，阅读位置保持不变。
- 保留用户排版设置与插图布局回归，补充真实绘制、图片命中和翻页期间刷新测试。

## English

### Reader layout and annotations

- The TXT and EPUB layout paths now share CJK line-start/line-end rules. Closing punctuation follows the preceding text without overrunning the safe margin, and paired punctuation or Latin words keep their natural spacing.
- Proactive annotations no longer reserve space in the body layout, so adding or removing one does not change line spacing, line height, or pagination. Generated annotations appear on the current page when the reader enters or resumes.
- Long-pressing an EPUB image opens a viewer with zoom, pan, rotation, reset, export, and source-location actions while preserving the reading position.
- User typography and artwork layout regressions remain covered, with additional real-paint, image-hit, and in-flight refresh checks.

### Upgrade and verification

- Version **1.1.2**, versionCode **76**, with a compatible database schema.
- The official Release uses the repository's release signing key. Performance builds use the development certificate for developer installs and are not release assets.
- `:app:testDebugUnitTest`, `:app:assembleRelease`, and `:app:assemblePerformance` completed locally: **1,508 tests, 1,500 passed, 8 conditional skips, 0 failures**.
- No physical device or paid model service was used for this verification. Automated checks do not replace device- and provider-specific testing.
