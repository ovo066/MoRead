# MoRead · 墨知 1.1.3

## 中文

### 阅读与查词

- 新增本地 MDX / MDD 词典管理和划线查词，支持词典原有图文排版、资源文件、生词收藏与 AI 释义。
- 新增中英对照、单段翻译、词下释义与音标，可独立选择翻译模型并保留已生成的译文。
- 新增「现代仿真」翻页，优化连续滑动、快速翻页和双页阅读体验；支持自定义阅读点击区域与物理翻页按键。
- 章节标题和高亮规则支持命名样式、实时预览、渐变文字及图片背景；阅读主题分别保存排版设置，正文净化支持预览后应用。

### 划线、笔记与伴读

- 新增「划线与笔记」回顾，支持按书籍、角色和内容筛选，以卡片或全屏方式翻阅，并可邀请伴读点评、共创和导出。
- 分享卡片支持自定义字体、配色、CSS 与高亮规则，可保存、编辑和复用模板。
- 人物资料支持属性、别名、关系和原文定位，并可提取为伴读角色卡。
- 伴读回复可按需显示 Token 用量和平均生成速度，改进部分 AI 服务的参数兼容性。
- 修复已设置的聊天背景图被默认底色遮挡的问题，改善深色模式下会话列表的文字对比度。

### 平板与界面

- 优化平板和可调整窗口布局，书架、设置、书籍详情、听书及伴读支持更合适的分栏与侧栏。
- 阅读工具、目录和人物页统一适配侧面板，改善滚动、切换页签及后台刷新时的位置稳定性。
- 优化阅读统计词云、书架搜索及阅读菜单，改善系统栏、屏幕切口和底部操作区的适配。

## English

### Reading and dictionaries

- Added local MDX / MDD dictionaries and selection lookup, with dictionary formatting, resource files, saved vocabulary, and AI definitions.
- Added bilingual reading, paragraph translation, inline glosses, and phonetics, with a separate translation model choice and saved translations.
- Added a modern page-curl effect and improved consecutive gestures, quick page turns, and two-page reading. Reading tap zones and physical page-turn keys are configurable.
- Chapter headings and highlighting rules now support named styles, live previews, gradient text, and image backgrounds. Reading themes retain their typography settings, and text cleanup can be previewed before applying.

### Highlights, notes, and companions

- Added a highlights and notes review with filters for books, companions, and content, card and full-screen views, companion commentary, co-writing, and export.
- Share cards support reusable templates with custom fonts, colors, CSS, and highlighting rules.
- Character profiles include attributes, aliases, relationships, and source links, and can be extracted into companion character cards.
- Companion replies can optionally display token usage and average generation speed. Improved parameter compatibility with some AI services.
- Fixed custom chat backgrounds being covered by the default page color and improved conversation-list text contrast in dark mode.

### Tablets and interface

- Improved tablet and resizable-window layouts, including columns and sidebars for the bookshelf, settings, book details, audiobook player, and companion chats.
- Reading tools, navigation, and character pages share adaptive side panels, with steadier scrolling, tab switching, and background updates.
- Refined reading-statistics word clouds, bookshelf search, reading menus, system-bar and display-cutout spacing, and bottom controls.
